# Privacy notice preparation

DRAFT REQUIREMENTS — not an effective Postqube privacy notice. Do not use this page
as a claim of compliance or submit it to Meta as a completed policy.

Before publication, identify the operator/legal entity and contact; applicable
Swiss/EU and other requirements; categories of account, OAuth, content and log data;
purposes and lawful bases; hosting/location; actual subprocessors; international
transfers; retention/deletion/backup periods; user rights/contact; cookies and
analytics; incident handling and policy changes. Describe whether AI content is
sent to a provider before enabling it. Never promise that all data stays on the VPS:
authorized social publishing and optional providers necessarily receive data.
