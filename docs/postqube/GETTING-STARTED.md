# Getting started

Postqube currently runs as a public beta at https://postqube.ownthecrown.lol.
Create your own account with email/password. Company is optional: a blank value
creates an internal workspace named Personal workspace. Do not share owner access.

1. Create an account at /auth.
2. Open the calendar at /launches.
3. Add your own X channel and authorize the provider.
4. Create a draft, review text/media and verify timezone before scheduling.
5. Instagram remains marked pending until the administrator completes Meta setup.

Password recovery/invitation emails do not currently work because no email provider
is configured. This is a launch blocker, not a promise to recover accounts by hand.
